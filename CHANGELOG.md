# Changelog

## 1.0.0 - 2026-10-06

First release.

- Surface map derived from the Tuttocitta Napoli atlas, plates 35-38
  (128x128 tiles, one atlas square = 16x16 tiles).
- Subterranean map beneath it: 16 shafts, aqueduct galleries under the
  streets, cisterns, quarry, shelter, metro and funicular tunnels.
- 64 jobs in four rioni, 20 troublemakers, the Parallelogram Force shield,
  the Decoro Index, the night clock, the atlas overlay, local score table.
- Three-layer parallax renderer (0.25x, 1x, 1.5x), 96 exact colours on RGB
  and indexed displays, torch light under ground.
- Five SID-like stereo tunes, panned effects, cave echoes.
- Host tests with a bot that plays a whole night; PRG32-QT and QEMU checks;
  Cartridge Store bundle.
